# Stefan White — Professional Speaker Website

## Goal
Create a polished, credible website that helps cruise lines, societies, universities, and event organisers quickly understand Stefan’s experience, previous work, specialist subjects, and suitability for an engagement.

## Website structure
- **Opening section:** Stefan’s portrait, his name, “Professional Speaker & Author,” a concise positioning statement, and clear links to explore his talks or make an enquiry.
- **Speaking credentials:** Highlight the verified figures from his CV — speaking on cruise ships since 2007, 112 enrichment talks, 326 destination talks, and 97 countries visited.
- **Previous work:** Present the organisations and settings named in the CV, including universities, the Royal Horticultural Society, leading societies, a private members group in the House of Commons, and cruise audiences.
- **Areas of expertise:** Organise his subjects into clear categories: history, arts and personalities, seas and oceans, climate change and conservation, golf, horticulture, and ports and destinations.
- **About Stefan:** Condense his entrepreneurial, travel, expedition, hotel-inspection, and speaking background into a warm professional biography.
- **Author section:** Feature the supplied cover of *Heroines of Horticulture*, its publication details, and his current writing projects.
- **Booking section:** End with a strong enquiry prompt for organisers. Since no contact details were supplied, use a clearly labelled contact placeholder rather than inventing information.

## Visual direction
- Create an original professional-speaker design rather than using the discarded concepts.
- Use Stefan’s supplied cruise portrait as the main first-screen image and the supplied book cover later on the page.
- Adopt an assured British editorial style with strong typography, generous spacing, restrained maritime blue, warm paper tones, and a subtle brass accent.
- Keep the experience highly readable on the current mobile view while expanding elegantly for desktop.
- Use restrained transitions and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Content safeguards
- Use only claims supported by the supplied CV.
- Do not invent testimonials, client logos, event dates, fees, talk titles, or contact details.
- Focus the personal biography on details relevant to Stefan’s professional credibility.
- Preserve the meaning of the source while tightening it for web readability.

## Technical details
- Replace the blank home page with a responsive single-page site using semantic sections and accessible navigation.
- Extract the portrait and book cover from the supplied document, store them through the project’s asset system, and provide descriptive alternative text.
- Add page-specific search and social metadata for Stefan White’s professional speaker website.
- Validate the finished page at mobile and desktop sizes, including navigation, links, image loading, text fit, and visual hierarchy.
